## 🛡️ Ad & Threat Interception

DNS Worker is engineered from the ground up as a **Protective DNS** resolver that safeguards your network, mobile devices, and IoT appliances at the internet's foundation:

- **Intrusive Ad & Telemetry Blocking**: Silently suppresses banner ads, video commercials, popups, and invasive operating system telemetry across iOS, Android, macOS, Windows, and Smart TVs without installing client software.
- **Proactive Threat Neutralization**: Real-time interception of malware distribution nodes, ransomware callbacks, phishing portals, cryptominers, and botnet Command & Control (C2) domains.
- **Dual-Layer Acceleration Engine**: Combines compact Bloom filters ($O(1)$ fast check) with memory Trie exact matching, capable of processing hundreds of thousands of rules with sub-millisecond evaluation latency and zero false positives.
- **Flexible Rule Management**: Pre-loaded with top-tier filter subscriptions (OISD Big, AdGuard Base, HaGeZi Multi PRO++, StevenBlack Hosts), alongside custom wildcard/regex matching and DNS rewrites.
- **Real-Time Audit & Logs**: Live inspection of permitted, blocked, and rewritten queries with an interactive drawer showing matched filter rules, client origin, and latency breakdown.
